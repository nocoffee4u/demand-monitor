## TL;DR
1. **The problem is real, independent, and specific to the left pad.** Rad's own Yotpo feed has **10 explicit complaints about the left "Rad UI Remote" screen, plus 1 probable**, from buyers other than the owner, across RadRover 6 Plus, RadCity 5 Plus and the Rad UI Remote replacement part. **6 of the 11 say "sun/sunlight/bright daylight" explicitly.** Another **4** say *both* screens are hard to read and **4** blame only the center display (3 of those look like defective units that were replaced). Pro reviewers say the same thing independently: Electric Bike Report (Rover 6 Plus), Electric Bike Review (RR3+ / Rover 6 Plus: "can only adjust the backlight brightness for the main center screen… not the left button pad screen… smoked plastic cover"), plus Rad's own manuals, which tell riders to tilt the remote "to prevent glare… test… in sunlight".
2. **The rate is low and the complaints are old.** That's 10 out of 5,963 dual-display reviews with text (**0.17%**). Counting every screen-visibility complaint, it's 23 (0.38%). **The newest left-pad complaint is from 2023-07-16.** There are **zero** in the 867 reviews from 2024–2025, and **zero among the 247 RadRunner 3 Plus reviews** (the owner's bike). The platform has been **discontinued** (none of these bikes are in Rad's current products.json). There is **no want-to-buy or DIY "hood" language** anywhere. The only DIY found is parking covers (socks, a shower cap) and hand-cupping.
3. **Nobody sells a glare hood for the Rad left pad.** Rad's catalog (148 US / 139 CA products) has no visor, shade, or display cover, and doesn't even list the replacement remote any more. What exists is **protective** covers. The closest is bubukiki's **"RadRover/City/Runner Remote UI Cover" at $14.99** (Etsy, for transport protection, 1 review). There are also clear PVC rain covers ($21.99, Amazon). Glare visors exist only for **other** brands' displays: **$12.99–$39.90** (Wired $27.99 with 7 five-star reviews, one saying "helpful to cut the glare"; Lectric $12.99/$19.90; EggRider $21–$24; Meet One $36–$39.90). Matte anti-glare protectors (a substitute) run $9.99–$37.98, and **none are Rad-specific**.
4. **Model fit correction.** Per two parts sellers' compatibility lists and the owner's manuals, the left pad with its own screen (Rad UI Remote) is on the **RadRunner 3 Plus (2023), RadRover 6 Plus HS/ST (2022–24, sold as RadRhino 6 Plus in the EU), and RadCity 5 Plus HS/ST (2022–24)**. The **RadTrike** also has a "Rad UI Remote" with a screen (its manual has the same glare-tilt text), but Rad lists it as a separate part ("Rad UI – RadTrike"), so shared geometry is NEEDS DATA. **The Radster Road/Trail does NOT have this pad.** Its left remote is 6 buttons with no screen, and it has a single color center display that reviewers call glare-free. The Radster should be removed from SUN-01's fit list. The older RadRunner Plus (single LCD, button-only pad) is excluded: 0 complaints in 1,856 reviews.
5. **Printability and liability are easy.** SUN-01 is (c), a non-load accessory: a tiny slip-over awning. It doesn't touch the throttle (which is on the right grip), the brake lever, or any structure. Design Bot already has `print_candidate_v1` (hood 73 x 30 x 54 mm, ASA). The risks are button access, clamp or brake-lever interference, retention, and heat (see §6).

## 3. Materials and fit claims
- **Rad covers (bubukiki):** "Slip it on, Snap it down"; optical plexi window on the center covers; the remote cover is black only. Fit disclaimers are common ("ONLY fits…", "DOES NOT FIT…"), and a 1★ "doesn't fit" review shows the fit risk.
- **LesFishing:** PETG "for weather and heat resistance rather than PLA".
- **NewDimensions3D (Printables):** TPU, plus a UI-remote clasp repair. The **UI remote bracket breaking** is a known issue (also a radowners.com thread, "Radrover 6 UI Remote Bracket Repair").

## 6. Liability and safety
- **Category (c).** It does **not** touch the throttle (half-twist, right grip), the brake levers, the brake hoses, the battery shell, or any structural part. It is **not** a launch-blocked part type.
- **Controls obstruction (must pass on the fit print):** (1) All four buttons stay fully pressable with a gloved thumb, including **power-off** (emergency stop) and hold-down walk mode. (2) The side wings must not reach the **left brake lever blade or its travel**, the bell, or the lever clamp. (3) It must not sit on the remote's **clamp bolts** (3 mm, 3 Nm) or stop the remote rotating for Rad's glare tilt. (4) It must not pinch the cable exit or strain relief (cable OD 3.96 mm; notch offset still an estimate).
- **Rider visibility:** the 10 mm awning sits below the eye line, so the risk is low. Use **matte black** so it doesn't bounce sun into the rider's eyes. **Instruct riders to ride with the lid stowed:** a closed lid hides PAS and battery while riding. The dual stowed-latches in the design address this.
- **Retention:** if it comes loose at speed, it can fall into the left-hand area near the brake lever or front wheel. Positive retention (friction plus latches) and a pull-off test are required. No adhesive on Rad's housing (warranty).
- **Heat/UV:** a Phoenix handlebar in summer exceeds PLA's softening point. **ASA only** (Design already specifies this; the Rev Bikes PLA+ shade is a cautionary contrast).
- **Claims:** no "waterproof" claim for the lid, no claim about sunglasses, and fitment stated per model ("RR3+ verified; Rover 6+/City 5+ same Rad UI Remote per parts compatibility lists, not SoMo-verified").

## Method lessons for the weekday hunt (free stack)
1. **Yotpo filter.json without `domain_key` searches the whole store** and returns each hit's product `domain_key` (the Shopify id). This is the way to find **discontinued** Rad SKUs (RR3+, Rover 6+, City 5+, Rad UI Remote/Display parts) whose product pages and products.json entries are gone. Save the id map in config (see the table in §1a).
2. **Neither star-by-star nor plain pagination alone is complete.** Yotpo pages return fewer than 150 items even when more exist. Loop **until a page comes back empty** (not until `len<150`), run both star-filtered and plain passes, and combine them by review id. Compare against `bottomline.total_review`.
3. **Use complaint rate and recency instead of all-time counts** for unmet_demand. SUN-01 saturates at 100 on Rover 6+ volume (4k reviews), but the real signal is 0.17%, all from 2022–2023, and 0 on the launch bike. Add fields `complaint_rate`, `last_complaint_date`, `launch_fit_complaints`.
4. **Verify hardware per model before listing fits.** The Radster uses a screenless remote, so it doesn't belong on SUN-01. The parts-seller compatibility lists (St George, Bikeshop West) are a fast free check.
5. **Separate the problem from the purchase.** Tag WTB/DIY phrases (`where can I buy`, `wish they sold`, `made my own`, `taped`, `cardboard`, `shade out of`, `sock over`) separately from complaint phrases, and require at least 1 WTB/DIY hit before DESIGN.
6. **Amazon /dp/ pages blocked today** while the SERP worked. Keep treating Amazon as flaky, and don't spend Context.dev credits on it (credits are depleted).
7. **EBR forum:** switch the weekday collector to `/search/search?keywords=` (`/search/1/?q=` is 404 now).
