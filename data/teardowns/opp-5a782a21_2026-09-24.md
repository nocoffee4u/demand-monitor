### How the OEM basket mounts (Rad's own pages)
- Large FMB: "**Mounts directly to the four front mounting points.**" Aluminum siding, wood bottom coated for water/UV, stainless fasteners. 13.6 x 21.2 x 6.4 in (34.6 x 53.6 x 16.2 cm). Weighs 5.6 lb. **Weight capacity 22 lb (10 kg).** The headlight moves to the basket front. (Product page, above.)
- Install guide: remove the head-tube bolts, place the basket over the **head-tube mounting points**, four **M6x20** bolts **torqued to 8 Nm**. On bikes with the brake line routed inside the fork (e.g., RadCity), you **reroute the brake cable** first. The headlight goes on the basket with an extension cable. Sources: https://radpowerbikes.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360056125294-Front-mounted-basket-installation-guide and https://manualspro.net/260167-rad-power-bikes-15-0277-001-front-mounted-basket-user-manual
- Because the basket is **frame (head-tube) mounted, it does not turn with the handlebars.** The front rack is mounted the same way, and EBR's RadRunner review praises that: it "won't influence steering or dump to the side when parking" (https://electricbikereview.com/rad-power-bikes/radrunner/).
- The head-tube bolt pattern is roughly **1 3/16 in x 3.5 in center-to-center (~30 x 89 mm)**, M6. This is forum-measured, not official: https://forums.electricbikereview.com/threads/rad-power-rack-mount-dimensions.30742/

## 5. SoMo differentiator and printable form
**H2S build volume: 340 mm x 320 mm x 340 mm**, per Bambu Lab's official page https://bambulab.com/en-us/h2s. The printer is not purchased yet.

| Option | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) The basket itself** | **NO (structural/liability)** | It carries up to 22 lb (Rad's rating) on the head tube, and reviews show loaded front baskets change handling and parked stability. At 346 x 536 mm outer, it's bigger than the H2S bed (536 > 340; 346 > 340), so it would have to be printed in pieces, which puts glued or bolted joints in the load path. FDM plastic creeps and fatigues under vibration and heat. The downside of a failure is a crash, and we'd be competing with $89–$120 aluminum anyway. |
| **(b) Mount / adapter / bracket for a third-party basket on Rad's 4-point head-tube mount** | **NO (structural/liability)** | This part *is* the load path, torqued to 8 Nm on M6 bolts. If it cracks, a loaded basket can drop onto the tire or fender while riding. The head-tube pattern (~30x89 mm) is also Rad-specific and has changed between years (an EBR user reports holes off by ~1/4 in between RadRover years). A free Cults STL already exists for the front-rack version. Do not sell. |
| **(c) Accessories for the basket** | **YES (the only safe lane)** | Non-load-path inserts that sit *inside* an OEM or aftermarket basket and carry no bike loads. **Ship-safe candidates:** (1) a divider/organizer insert for the Large FMB / Large Basket (the "big and empty, stuff slides and bounces" complaint); (2) a small-item floor tray / mesh-gap panel for the Small FMB ("gaping holes … small items"); (3) a drop-in cup/bottle holder. **Gated candidate:** a headlight aim-correction or offset bracket, answering the "light shines 20–30° right", "light doesn't follow steering", and "hitting headlight" complaints. It is low-load but safety-relevant (lighting, near the front wheel), so it needs a hardware/material review before launch, and free MakerWorld versions plus a $6.49 Etsy analog cap the price. **Not printable:** liners and rain lids (fabric; Rad sells a $65.25 liner, Line and Ride $34.99; a 536 mm lid won't fit the bed without seams). |

**Differentiator, if (c) moves forward:** Rad-specific fit on its exact baskets (Large FMB 13.6x21.2 in, Small FMB 10.6x13.7 in, Large Basket 12x19.5 in), which universal organizers don't have. Liner makers already use a "measure first, fits Rad SMALL only" positioning (Line and Ride). Demand for (c) is **not proven**: none of the want-to-buy language found is about dividers or trays. The demand signal is for the *basket* being in stock.

## Method lessons (to make this repeatable in the weekday hunt, free tools only)
1. **Rad's Yotpo widget JSON is the best free review source:** `https://api-cdn.yotpo.com/v1/widget/<store_key>/products/<shopify_product_id>/reviews.json?per_page=150&page=N`. It gives star distribution plus full review text, with no login and no bot wall. Product IDs come straight from products.json. Add a `rad_reviews` collector that, for every OOS OEM SKU, pulls the 1–3★ text and keyword-tags it (size, steer, cables, light, install, water, noise).
2. **Always check Rad's in-stock substitutes before scoring unmet demand.** products.json tags (`Category_*`, `Current Model Compatibility_*`) let you auto-find same-category, same-compatibility SKUs that are available. For this card that would have caught Small FMB and Front Rack + Large Basket. Add a `substitute_in_stock` flag that caps unmet_demand.
3. **Amazon via plain curl + desktop UA worked today** for both the SERP and /dp/ pages. The title, price, availability, rating, bullets, "Top reviews" block, and AI summary are all parseable. Treat it as flaky: record OK/BLOCKED per run, and never fill gaps by guessing.
4. **Etsy v3 auth changed:** the header must be `KEYSTRING:SHARED_SECRET`. Update `marketplace_scan.py` / the weekday Etsy step.
5. **YouTube Data API commentThreads** is cheap and gives comment text for the demand videos; use it instead of yt-dlp (the yt-dlp path at `/workspace/yt-dlp` is a symlink into `.venv`, so call `/workspace/.venv/bin/yt-dlp` if needed).
6. **Liability triage belongs in the teardown template:** classify every printable idea as (a) product, (b) load-path adapter, or (c) non-load accessory before scoring. Auto-DROP (a)/(b) for anything on the steering/head-tube/fork/rack load path.
7. Keep citing the H2S build volume from https://bambulab.com/en-us/h2s (340x320x340 mm) and flag any part with a dimension over 320–340 mm as needing multiple pieces.
